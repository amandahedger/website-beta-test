FIELD OBSERVATIONS

NOTES | TAKEAWAYS

Everywhere I go, I come back changed in some way. Explore what's worth seeing, doing, and what I learned along the way.

STOP 1 | LONDON 51.5074° N, 0.1278° W

London | Make sure you look for cars on the correct side of the road.

Sights & Excursions

Take a ride on the London Eye to catch a view of Big Ben, then hop on the double decker bus accross Tower Bridge to get the tube to the West End. Catch a show or explore the various food options offered before heading to the buckingham palace to see the Royal Guards. Camden Market is always a cool stop before shopping at Primark and getting Nando's for tea.

WHAT CAME HOME WITH ME

While this wasn't my first international trip, it was the first I had planned out and the reason I got my passport. My first time in London was for a convention where I met friends I made online in person for the first time. We all immediately clicked and I quickly learned I had found community, something I had longed for prior. Having this experience helped me realize the potential I had to go farther than I could ever imagine. Experiencing convetions also opened my eyes to a completely different world that brought an immense amount of joy and wonder where I had previously lost it.

STOP 2 | VANCOUVER 49.246292° N, -123.116226° W

Vancouver | Where city meets forest meets ocean.

Sights & Excursions

Public transport to the Capilano Suspension Bridge. (Bonus points if it's during the holidays to see the setup) On the way back down you can either detour to Lynn Creek for a hike in the mountains or go over Lions Gate Bridge into Stanley Park. Walk around the Seawall towards Canada Place and peak into Gastown to see the iconic Clock. English Bay sunsets are an unreal experience and if you really want to venture... Steveston where the show "Once Upon a Time" was filmed, Granville Island Market, or Barnet Marine Park are my go-to areas for scenic views.

WHAT CAME HOME WITH ME

Vancouver will always keep a large piece of me as the first time I went was planned quickly and last minute. Between delayed flights, weather scares, and anything in between I learned to trust that you will get where you need to go on your time. This destination was my first time leaving the country and I had no idea what to prepare for. I am eternally grateful for the leap of faith that changed the trajectory of traveling for me.

STOP 3 | SALEM 42.5195° N, -70.8967° W

Salem | Beware the witches in town.

Sights & Excursions

Full of memorials and historic in nature, you obviously can't go wrong with the Salem Witch Museum. Down the street you will find a dedicated cemetery to the victims of the Witch Trials where generations of people gather to pay respects and leave momentos at the site. Just down the street you can enter the Salem Witch House and if I do say so myself the energy is heavy. You can't leave without making a stop at the harbour to see the House of Seven Gables and make a pit stop at the iconic Hocus Pocus House.

WHAT CAME HOME WITH ME

Salem was a lesson for me do lean into topics that interest me. I have always been intrigued by history but especially the Witch Trials because of how heavily it plays a role in Halloween (one of my favorite holidays). Spending the weekend immersed in spooky season during spring was fulfilling. It was also a birthday trip, so taking the leap to go on a quick trip for myself was a quick refresh and reminder that even the smallest getaways can leave a lasting impact.

STOP 4 | PORTLAND 45.5231° N, 122.6765° W

Portland | Watch out for Bigfoot.

Sights & Excursions

Peace, love, and rain... lots of rain. Don't let it stop you from venturing out to Music Millennium, and see St. John's Bridge (yes, the one featured in Wildwood) then off to all local things Twilight. From the Bella's House to Carver Cafe aka the iconic Diner you can put yourself right in the middle of your own PNW experience. Make sure you stop at Multnomah Falls and hear the waterfall crash on the surface and take a detour about an hour southeast to the town of Florence and get a real hidden beach experience. There's nothing quite like seeing the mountains and the beach at the same time.

WHAT CAME HOME WITH ME

Oregon is home to some of my family, so making routine trips out to visit them are always cherished and remind me the people that mean most know no distance. Every visit it's as if no time has passed and we always know to spend the time together intentionally as we don't get much of it. It's a gentle reminder that distance may keep us apart most days of the year but they will always be worth the time to travel to get to make memories.

STOP 5 | NASSAU/CASTAWAY CAY 26.0923° N, 77.5365° W

Nassau/Castaway Cay | Please remember to pack enough sunscreen.

Sights & Excursions

Nassau is full of little shops and cute cafe's for different food options. Whether you are just browsing the streets for the views or doing more intentional shopping there is a little something for everyone to enjoy. Making your way to Castaway Cay... well you have to be taken on a Disney Cruise Ship. Once you make it you get to enjoy the entire day on this private island, food, drinks, and views included. Go out early to run the Castaway Cay 5K or take a walk around the island and you'll find the one and only runway for aircraft to land and if you venture far enough you'll find the lookout tower where you can spot your ship docked and sitting peacefully awaiting your return.

WHAT CAME HOME WITH ME

Disney always knows how to spread the magic to everyone who dares embrace it. Being on my first cruise ship and it being Disney made it to the expectations are high. This entire exursion made me feel like I was a child again experiencing the wonder of everything the world around me had to offer, something we often forget or lose as we get older. Having this experience and enjoying the peace of a private island helped me recenter and realize I can embrace joy and wonder no matter where I'm at in life.

STOP 6 | EDINBURGH 55.9533° N, 3.1883° W

Edinburgh | The caslte might actually be haunted.

Sights & Excursions

Take the train anywhere in Scotland and you can't go wrong, but Edinburg has quite a few stops like the Edinburg Castle, Crammond Falls or the National Galleries of Scotland that are must-sees. Rumor has it the castle is haunted, and while you can book tours the exterior is just as magnificant. If you're a Harry Potter fan you will definitely want to catch the Jacobite Steam Train that the Hogwarts express is inspired by or stroll down the well known Victoria Street. Botanical Gardens, golf courses, or the Scotish Higlands are all great options to soak in the Scottish experience.

WHAT CAME HOME WITH ME

While in Scotland I found reconnecting with nature around me was something that made me extremely happy. It was walking along the coast and reuniting with friends that live in the UK (yes, the same ones from our London stop) that once again distance can't separate friends and seeing a different part of the world will never get old. As I have broadened my horizons over the years, I have come to realize each new place continues to piece together me as a person and only adds to my admiration and appreciation for different walks of life and cultures.
